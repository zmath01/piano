How to read these annotations +

Score context describes a published musical form. Computed cues match eight consecutive notes with no internal overlap beyond 0.025 quarter-note beats. Semitone intervals and onset patterns must agree with the displayed transform; note lengths allow 0.025 beats of release/articulation tolerance. They do not certify complete subjects or all fugal devices. Overlapping matched prefixes are labelled stretto-like, rather than a complete stretto analysis. Tonal answers, ornamentation and polyphonic part encoding can prevent detection; no match does not mean no imitation.

Retrograde is available below as an experiment. The app does not automatically claim that the selected composition uses retrograde, Fibonacci proportions, a golden ratio, or hidden numerical messages.

EXPLORATION, NOT A SCORE CLAIM

The mathematical relationships +

Pitch translation Tₖ(p) = p + k composes by addition: TₐTᵦ = Tₐ₊ᵦ. Pitch reflection Iₐ(p) = 2a − p is an involution: Iₐ² = identity. For a note occupying [q, q + d], retrograde maps its onset to L − q − d and keeps d; applying it twice restores the original interval. Time dilation Dₛ(q, d) = (sq, sd) composes by multiplication: DᵣDₛ = Dᵣₛ. Dilation is not a distance-preserving isometry.

These are transformations relating motifs. They do not imply that an entire composition stays unchanged under them. Pitch classes may be considered modulo 12; the app plays in 12-tone equal temperament, which is not established as Bach’s historical tuning by the title Well-Tempered Clavier.

THE INSTRUMENT

A little room
for music.

An 88-key piano, built with Three.js and Web Audio. Play with your mouse, multiple fingers, or your computer keyboard. On a small screen, use the register arrows to explore the piano.

Keyboard: A S D F G H J K play the white keys; W E T Y U play the black keys. Z / X change register. Hold Space for sustain, or toggle the Sustain button. Opening this panel pauses the sample.

The library: 153 tracks cover both WTC books, The Art of Fugue, the Goldberg Variations and BWV 617. The study panel separates published context, computed eight-note matches and exploratory transformations. Open Edition, credits and sources for each track’s attribution and license.
