# Salamander Grand Piano v3 recordings

Alexander Holm recorded a Yamaha C5 acoustic grand piano. These adapted samples are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The recordings have their own license alongside the application's MIT license and the separate score licenses. [Original release](https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3) · [FLAC source revision](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/tree/3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404).

The filenames give the MIDI pitch. The bank uses velocity layer 8 from A0, C1 through C7, F♯1 through F♯7, and C8. The left microphone channel was resampled to 32 kHz and encoded as mono MP3 at 64 kb/s. The samples were aligned to the attack, gain-balanced, trimmed to about 4–10 seconds, and faded at the tail. Playback uses the nearest recording with semitone adjustment, velocity gain, and register-dependent EQ.
