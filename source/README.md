# Piano · Bach counterpoint explorer

Open **index.html** in a browser. The single file embeds the complete library,
Three.js, synthesized piano sound and interactive analysis. It works offline.
Tap Play or a piano key to enable browser audio.

## Library: 153 tracks

- WTC I: all 24 prelude–fugue pairs, 48 tracks.
- WTC II: all 24 pairs plus two alternative prelude readings, 50 tracks.
- Art of Fugue: 22 tracks, including four canons, both mirror forms, the
  two-keyboard versions and the surviving unfinished final fugue.
- Goldberg: Aria, all 30 variations and Aria da capo, 32 tracks. Da capo reuses
  the opening Aria sequence.
- The original BWV 617 sample is retained.

WTC II preludes 1 and 24 have labelled Altnikol/autograph versions. Art of Fugue
retains this edition's numbering: XVI/XVII are the mirror pairs often called
XII/XIII; XIX is the unfinished final fugue often called XIV. No completion is
invented. Repeat realizations follow the individual source MIDI editions.

## Interactive study

Choose a collection and movement, then play, pause, seek or change tempo.
Recorded MIDI parts have distinct colors and mute/Solo controls. These parts
may combine notated voices; they are not an invented complete voice separation.
The note timeline follows playback. Select a timed motif match to jump to it
or loop its fragment. Compare pair switches rectus/inversus mirror versions.

The Symmetry lab plots and auditions pitch translation, inversion, augmentation,
diminution and retrograde. Reference/transform auditions pause the full piece
and use quarter-note = 120. The Goldberg canon ladder links variations and
distinguishes canons, the fughetta and the quodlibet.

Score context, computed matches and exploratory models are labelled separately.
The 429 detections establish eight-note prefixes, not complete subjects or a
comprehensive fugue analysis. Tonal answers and ornamented imitations may not
match the strict chromatic detector. If no safe prefix is found, the lab clearly
uses a separate generated teaching motif. See ANALYSIS.md for the method.

## Live piano

Touch/click the keys; drag for a glissando. A S D F G H J K play white keys;
W E T Y U play black keys. Z/X and the register arrows move the keyboard.
Hold Space or latch Sustain for live notes. Accessible keys provides semantic
buttons: Tab to a note and hold Enter. Leaving the tab pauses the piece and
releases live/demonstration voices.

Playback is synthesized piano in 12-tone equal temperament, not a recording.
The WTC title does not establish Bach's historical tuning as equal temperament.
Original MIDI pitches, velocities, durations, tempo changes and register
doublings are retained; timing is rounded to milliseconds. There is one documented
unpaired note-off in Goldberg 15, with no sounding note-on removed.

On Android, use a browser rather than a text preview. If needed, serve the folder:

```sh
cd path/to/piano
python -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:8765 on the same device; Ctrl+C stops the server.

## Source and validation

TRACKS.md lists each source and license. Editable files, original MIDIs, LilyPond
editions and retrieval manifests are in source/. Standard-library Python rebuild:

```sh
python3 source/make_library.py
python3 source/build.py
```

The rebuilt app is source/dist/index.html. Documentation is generated by
finalize_v2.py from library metadata. No Node packages are required for use or
building. Verification reports and captures are in verification/; development
checks use Node and Playwright. Local backup archives are not distributed.

All 153 track loads, timed cue following/looping, part isolation, mirror switching
and transformed auditions were checked. Every note and cue was independently
compared with the source MIDI. Desktop/mobile rendering was checked using the
Canvas 2D fallback, which paints the same Three.js geometry when WebGL fails.
Native WebGL appearance and physical speaker output remain unverified here.
