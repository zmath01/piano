# Public distribution

This distribution contains the latest Bach piano, editable sources, music data,
licenses and verification evidence. Local backups and duplicate ZIP archives
are omitted. Device paths and contact email fields have been removed from this
copy while retaining third-party copyright/editor attribution and source URLs.
Source-asset hashes identify upstream originals; the public MANIFEST.json hashes
the actual sanitized files. The original local delivery is left unchanged.

The creator explicitly requested publication of the two payment QR images from
the zmath01/wechat-export repository. Those images are unmodified; their source
and hashes are recorded in source/assets/support/sources.json. No payment API,
account password, access token or private key is included. Actual transfers are
handled by the visitor's payment application; no test payment was performed.
